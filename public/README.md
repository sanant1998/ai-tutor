## hero-desk.jpg

The hero's right panel is layered: the photograph sits at the back, and the
plan card, notebook page and margin notes are drawn on top of it. Until the
file exists, a warm gradient and paper grain stand in — the composition reads
as finished either way, so this is graceful degradation rather than a bug.

Shoot or source a warm, bright desk scene: a stack of textbooks with visible
spines, a small potted plant, a mug, and an open spiral notebook, lit from the
left, with generous empty space in the upper-left third where the plan card
sits. Landscape, roughly 5:4.
